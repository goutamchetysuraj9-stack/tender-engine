1. Information Aggregator Status (Data Ka Shrot)

Tender Engine par dikhaya gaya saara data automated web scrapers ke zariye public state procurement portals (jaise assamtenders.gov.in) se collect kiya jata hai. Hum data me koi badlav nahi karte. Users se nivedan hai ki kisi bhi contract ko apply karne se pehle original portal par validity check karein.

2. Third-Party Loan & Financing Links Liability

Hamaray dashboard par contractors ki suvidha ke liye RBI-registered ya commercial funding gateways ke references aur affiliate hyperlinks diye gaye hain.

  • Hum koi broker, official loan provider, ya financial advisors nahi hain.
  • Aapke aur kisi bhi teesri-paksh (third-party) financial company ke beech hone wale kisi bhi samjhauta, dastavez, ya kagazi jhamela me hamara koi haath nahi hoga.
  • Kisi bhi processing fees ko pay karne se pehle company ke authorization aur physical tracking credentials ki jaanch swayam karein.

3. Zero Financial Liability Clause

Kisi bhi tarah ke online financial fraud, vyavasayik nuksan (business losses), ya transaction disputes ke liye yeh portal, iske database networks, aur iske owners/developers kanooni roop se 100% mukt hain aur koi daindari (liability) sweekar nahi karte.
